Our Specializations

Expert Ayurvedic Treatments & Therapies

Comprehensive traditional healthcare services for holistic healing and wellness

Panchakarma Therapy

Complete detoxification and rejuvenation through traditional five-fold therapy including Vaman, Virechana, Basti, Nasya, and Shirodhara.

Infertility Treatment

Specialized care for primary and secondary infertility, PCOD, and reproductive health using natural Ayurvedic methods and therapies.

Orthocare

Natural treatment for joint pain, cervical pain, low back pain, arthritis, and other musculoskeletal disorders.

Gastrocare

Comprehensive digestive health solutions for acidity, constipation, IBS, liver disorders, and other gastrointestinal issues.

Anorectal Care

Effective treatment for piles, fissures, fistula, and other anorectal disorders using gentle Ayurvedic approaches.

Special Treatments

Unique therapies including Marma therapy, Neuro therapy, skin care, hair fall treatment, and navel seating.
